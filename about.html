About TeachOn

We're a team of teachers — not just one.

TeachOn is built around a group of professional teachers, each matched to students based on how they learn best. Whether it's a home visit in Agartala or a live class online, every session is led by a teacher from our team — chosen to fit your child, not the other way around.

How we work

Two teams, one goal — the right teacher for every child.

We run two sides of the same organisation: an offline team for home tuition in Agartala, and an online team for students anywhere else. Both are held to the same standard.

🏠

Offline — Home Tuition

We're affiliated with a network of qualified teachers based in Agartala, Tripura. Once we understand what your child needs, we send a matched teacher to your home for one-on-one or small-group sessions.

  • Teachers vetted and coordinated by TeachOn
  • Matched to your child's class, board, and subjects
  • Sessions held at your home, on a schedule you agree on
💻

Online — Anywhere in India or Abroad

Separately, we have a dedicated team of online teachers who take live classes over video call — for students outside Agartala, or anyone who simply prefers learning from home.

  • One-on-one or group online batches
  • Same subject coverage as offline, Class 1–12
  • No location limits — India or abroad
Why a team

One tutor can't be everything. A team can.

Every child learns differently, and every subject needs a different kind of teaching. Instead of stretching one tutor across every class and subject, TeachOn coordinates a group of teachers — so your child gets someone genuinely suited to them.

🎯

Matched, not assigned

We consider your child's class, board, subjects, and preferred style before choosing who teaches them.

📍

Local where it matters

Our offline teachers know Agartala's schools and boards, so home tuition stays relevant to what your child is studying.

🌐

Unlimited by location, online

Our online team means distance is never a reason your child can't get the right teacher.
