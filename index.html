Two ways to learn

Home tuition, or a batch — you choose.

Both are led by professional teachers matched to your child. The difference is pace, price, and how much one-on-one time your child gets.

🏠

Home Tuition

In Agartala, a matched teacher comes to your house for focused, one-on-one sessions. Anywhere else in India or abroad, the same one-on-one attention is available online.

Privacy & Terms

Privacy Policy

When you fill in the enquiry form or quick-question box, we collect the parent/guardian name, student name, class, school name, board, phone number, and any message you choose to add.

This information is used only to respond to your enquiry and arrange lessons. This site has no server or database — form details go straight to our WhatsApp or email inbox on your own device when you tap Send, and are not stored on any website backend, sold, or shared with third parties.

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

To ask a question about your data, or to request it be deleted from our records, use the contact form above.

Terms of Use
